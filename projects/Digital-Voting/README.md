# Digital Voting — Jordan Smart Elections

**Election Process Design | Digital Support | Pilot Planning**

## Overview

This project explores how digital tools could support transparent counting, verification and communication of election results.

The work includes two distinct proposals: a broader smart-election vision prepared for presentation through a political party, and a bounded paper-based pilot concept for internal elections and civic education.

## My Contribution

I developed the concept documents, proposed technical architecture, election-day scenarios and phased pilot approach.

I also prepared outreach materials and communicated the project through LinkedIn posts and correspondence with international institutions.

## Strategic Concept

The broader proposal explores:

- Optical ballot recognition and local processing.
- Proposed cryptographic verification and audit records.
- Aggregated participation and results dashboards.
- A phased development pathway.

AI, blockchain and advanced cryptographic components remain proposed options requiring specialist assessment and testing.

## Paper-Based Pilot Concept

The Jordan Smart Elections concept defines a narrower workflow:

1. Voters mark paper ballots.
2. Optical scanning produces preliminary counts.
3. A controlled tally process consolidates results.
4. Manual counting checks digital outputs.
5. A read-only dashboard displays approved aggregate results.

Paper ballots remain the authoritative record. Equipment failure triggers a return to manual counting.

This pilot concept does not include online ballot casting.

## Proposed Pilot Settings

- Simulations using dummy ballots.
- Student or youth council elections.
- Internal elections within participating organisations.

Any pilot requires host approval, agreed procedures and a defined evaluation plan.

## Proposed Safeguards

- Separation of voter identity from ballot choices.
- Restricted access to tally records and operational logs.
- Documented handling of unclear ballots and discrepancies.
- Secure retention of paper records.
- Manual verification and fallback procedures.

## Communication and Outreach

Outreach has included contacts with institutions in Estonia and Singapore and the Friedrich Naumann Foundation.

Correspondence does not establish endorsement, partnership, procurement or approval.

## Project Stage

Concept development, technical documentation and outreach.

The materials do not establish a deployed election system, completed pilot, independently tested security or approval for public elections.

## Professional Relevance

This work demonstrates project design, technical requirements coordination, process documentation, pilot planning and institutional communication.
