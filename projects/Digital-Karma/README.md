# Digital Karma

**Institutional Performance and Accountability Concept**

## Overview

Digital Karma is a proposed framework for monitoring institutional performance through operational indicators, trend analysis and management reporting.

The concept explores how more frequent evidence-based reviews could support institutional accountability and service improvement.

## My Contribution

I developed the project concept and executive summary, explored an institutional performance scoring approach, and communicated the idea through LinkedIn posts and outreach to the organisation أرض.

## Proposed Components

- Integration of selected institutional data sources.
- Defined performance indicators and reporting periods.
- Analysis of performance changes and trends.
- A central dashboard for authorised institutional reviewers.
- A proposed institutional Digital Trust Score.

## Institutional Digital Trust Score

The proposed score would summarise selected aspects of institutional performance.

Its methodology, indicator weights, data-quality requirements and review procedures need to be defined and tested before scores could support decisions.

The concept concerns institutional performance, not a personal social-credit system.
