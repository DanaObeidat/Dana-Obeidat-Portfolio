# White Petrol

**Industrial project design for assessing difficult hydrocarbon streams**

Developed by Dana Obeidat

## Overview

White Petrol is a documented project design for assessing selected difficult and circular hydrocarbon streams before treatment, blending or scale decisions.

The proposed system connects material identity, sampling, compatibility screening, treatment-route evaluation, contaminant tracking and review responsibilities.

Its central question is:

**What evidence is required before a material stream can proceed to testing, treatment or further development?**

## Proposed Assessment Workflow

1. Establish the stream source, identity and intended use.
2. Define sampling and chain-of-custody requirements.
3. Characterise the material and identify evidence gaps.
4. Assess compatibility, operational exposure and safety requirements.
5. Evaluate candidate treatment routes.
6. Plan controlled testing and track outputs and residual streams.
7. Review results against defined acceptance criteria.
8. Record whether to proceed, modify, hold, re-route or reject.

Progression depends on the evidence required for each decision.

## Main Design Components

### Feedstock Assessment

The documentation defines a Feedstock Visa framework and stream passport for recording material identity, characteristics, restrictions and assessment status.

### Treatment and Output Review

Candidate routes are evaluated against stream characteristics, compatibility, operating requirements and output-quality criteria.

Treatment activity alone does not establish that an output is suitable for a buyer or intended application.

### Environmental and Safety Accountability

The design includes requirements for contaminant fate, mass balance, spent media, wastewater, sludge and other residual streams, together with site-specific safety review and stop conditions.

### Data and Decision Support

The proposed digital architecture covers evidence records, data lineage, model governance, uncertainty and human review.

AI functions are documented as proposed decision support and require measured data and validation before operational use.

### Pilot and Financial Planning

The project includes pilot design, site requirements, testing protocols, acceptance criteria, independent-review requirements and financial scenario models.

Financial outputs remain illustrative until supported by quotations, site conditions and measured pilot results.

## Project Status

White Petrol is at the documented concept, system-design and pilot-planning stage.

The portfolio materials do not establish completed industrial trials, verified treatment performance, regulatory approval, commercial deployment or realised financial returns.

Laboratory testing, pilot execution, independent verification and site-specific engineering remain necessary to evaluate the proposed pathways.
