# Central Bank of Personal Data

**Institutional Design | Data Governance | Data Valuation Concepts**

## Overview

The Central Bank of Personal Data is a proposed institutional framework connecting personal data rights, governance, controlled licensing and economic valuation.

The concept explores how individuals could exercise greater control over the use of their data within a proposed national governance structure.

## My Contribution

I developed the project concept, executive summaries and proposed institutional leadership model. I also prepared an initial data valuation table to explore relationships between data categories, potential uses and indicative values.

## Proposed Institutional Model

The concept defines two complementary leadership roles:

- **Sovereign Chief Data Officer:** Data governance, policy coordination and valuation frameworks.
- **Sovereign Chief Banking Architect:** Exploration of proposed data-backed economic instruments and institutional financing models.

These are proposed roles within the concept, not established public appointments.

## Proposed Operating Components

- Individual dashboards for managing data access and permissions.
- Controlled licensing of specified data uses.
- Governance and accountability arrangements.
- Secure infrastructure and privacy technologies for technical assessment.
- A proposed marketplace connecting permitted uses with participating organisations.

## Data Valuation Exploration

An initial draft compares data categories, potential applications and illustrative values in JOD and USD.

The figures are exploratory assumptions. They are not validated market prices, an approved exchange rate or evidence of completed transactions.

Further development requires a defined pricing unit, valuation methodology, currency treatment and supporting evidence.

## Project Stage

Concept development and institutional design.

The materials describe proposed functions and technologies. They do not establish an operating bank, regulatory approval, verified legal compliance, issued financial instruments or implemented infrastructure.

## Professional Relevance

This work demonstrates concept development, institutional structuring, governance planning and executive communication across policy, technology and economic questions.
