# YWIG — Your Waste Is Gold

**Solid and Liquid Waste | Traceability, Material Intelligence and Evidence Controls**

[View work sample (PDF)](Dana%20Obeidat%20YWIG%20Work%20Sample.pdf)



## Overview

YWIG is a project design for connecting waste-source records, operational events, samples, laboratory results and qualification decisions.

It covers solid and liquid waste through separate workflows, with stream-specific sampling, testing, acceptance criteria and recovery requirements.

## My Contribution

I developed the project concept, technical architecture, operating workflows, sample-integrity controls and pilot framework.

The documentation connects field records with laboratory evidence, material assessment and accountable decisions.

## Solid Waste Pathway

- Source, stream and batch identification.
- Operational event and handover records.
- Sampling and chain-of-custody controls.
- Material characterisation and contamination assessment.
- Evidence requirements for potential reuse or recovery.

## Liquid Waste Pathway

- Source and liquid-stream identification.
- Sampling, preservation and custody requirements.
- Stream-specific laboratory testing.
- Contamination and suitability assessment.
- Evidence requirements for treatment, recovery or disposal decisions by qualified parties.

## Technical and Operational Design

- Edge and cloud architecture for field-event capture.
- Linked site, stream, batch, sample and laboratory records.
- Quality checks, exceptions and audit trails.
- Material Passport records for eligible streams.
- Pilot acceptance criteria and evidence exports.

Material Passports document the evidence and limitations supporting a decision. They do not replace laboratory conclusions, regulatory approvals or buyer acceptance.

## Project Stage

Concept development, technical and operational documentation, and pilot planning.

Illustrative records and simulation cases demonstrate the proposed workflow. They do not establish live deployment, treatment performance or commercial results.

## Professional Relevance

This work demonstrates capabilities in environmental project design, process documentation, pilot planning, quality controls and coordination between site teams, licensed operators and laboratories.
