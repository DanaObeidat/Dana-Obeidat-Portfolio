## Overview

Talent Exchange brings together two related project concepts:

- **TSX — Talent Sovereign Exchange:** A proposed framework for mapping skills, identifying workforce needs and connecting talent with opportunities across sectors.
- **TMX — Talent Medical Exchange:** A healthcare-focused talent exchange concept.

## TSX — Multisector Talent Intelligence

The concept explores how institutions could connect:

- Skills and workforce information.
- Sector-specific talent requirements.
- International demand and opportunity mapping.
- Partnership and talent-mobility pathways.

Proposed sectors include healthcare, artificial intelligence and climate-related industries.

## TMX — Medical Talent Exchange

TMX represents the healthcare-focused direction within the portfolio.

Its detailed operating model, credential-verification process, institutional responsibilities and placement requirements need to be documented separately before implementation claims can be made.

## My Contribution

I developed the strategic concept and proposed phased development model, connecting workforce mapping with institutional partnerships and potential cross-border opportunities.

## Proposed Development Path

1. Define the target sector, users and workforce information requirements.
2. Establish partner responsibilities and candidate participation conditions.
3. Test a limited workflow with agreed review criteria.
4. Evaluate the evidence before expanding the scope.

## Project Stage

Concept and strategic planning.

The available summary describes intended capabilities. It does not establish a functioning platform, validated AI models, signed institutional partnerships or completed placements.

## Professional Relevance

This work illustrates strategic project development, stakeholder mapping, programme structuring and cross-border coordination planning.
