# GIG — Grid of Inherent Geodata

**Project strategy, technical architecture and decision governance**  
Developed by Dana Obeidat

## Overview

GIG is a proposed Pre-Failure Coordinate Intelligence Layer for organising location-specific evidence and supporting inspection, verification and intervention priorities.

The design connects terrain, drainage, ground conditions, infrastructure exposure and operational records within a traceable decision workflow.

## The Design Challenge

Relevant information can be distributed across maps, maintenance logs, inspection reports, historical records and field observations.

GIG addresses the coordination question: what does the available evidence support at a particular location, what remains uncertain, and who should act next?

## Central Work Product

The **Coordinate Evidence Card** brings together:

- Location and asset context
- Evidence sources, dates and quality
- Observed signals and conflicting records
- Uncertainty and missing evidence
- Permitted next action
- Responsible reviewer and decision authority
- Review history and verification results

The proposed decision pathway is:

**Watch → Verify → Intervene → Escalate**

These states guide review and action. They do not represent guaranteed failure predictions.

## Proposed Pilot

The documented pilot proposes one defined area, approximately 50–100 coordinates and 5–7 initial data sources.

Evaluation would examine evidence traceability, reviewer consistency, prioritisation usefulness, verification findings and operational handoff.

Scoring weights and thresholds require calibration and testing.

## Development Status

**Documented conceptual, technical and governance design, with a proposed pilot.**

The materials presented here do not establish completed site deployment, validated predictive performance, independent certification or implemented integrations.

GIG is intended to support qualified professionals and asset owners. Engineering, hydrological, geotechnical, environmental and laboratory conclusions remain subject to the relevant specialist verification.
