## Overview

Green Shale is a documented project design for evaluating selected oil-shale-derived and associated mineral materials for potential industrial applications.

The platform connects material origin, process history, sampling, laboratory characterisation, environmental assessment, product testing and buyer requirements.

Its central question is:

**What evidence is needed to move a specific material stream from technical potential to an acceptable industrial application?**

## Proposed Application Pathways

Each pathway requires its own material evidence, testing programme and acceptance criteria.

- **GreenCem:** assessment of mineral inputs for cementitious and construction applications.
- **ZeoWater and Shale Adsorbents:** evaluation of candidate media for water treatment and adsorption.
- **AmmoniaGuard:** evaluation of mineral-based materials for ammonia mitigation.
- **EcoBoard and Carbon Pavers:** assessment of candidate inputs for construction products.
- **Agricultural pathways:** evaluation of suitability, environmental restrictions and field performance where relevant.

These are proposed development pathways, not validated products.

## Qualification Workflow

1. Establish material source and process history.
2. Define representative sampling and chain of custody.
3. Characterise composition, variability and relevant properties.
4. Assess environmental behaviour and residual burdens.
5. Identify the applicable testing and qualification route.
6. Test performance against application-specific requirements.
7. Evaluate repeatability through controlled pilot work.
8. Obtain independent review and buyer acceptance where required.
9. Decide whether to advance, redesign, hold or stop.

A published result for another material source does not establish suitability for the candidate stream.

## Phased Development Plan

### Phase 1 — Validation and Pathway Selection

Characterise materials, screen applications, define test requirements, assess buyer needs and examine cost assumptions.

### Phase 2 — Product Qualification and Industrial Pilots

Advance selected pathways through repeat testing, pilot evaluation, supplier coordination and application-specific conformity review.

### Phase 3 — Conditional Scale and Integration

Consider expansion only after technical performance, environmental requirements, operating costs and commercial acceptance are sufficiently supported.

## Project Status and Evidence Boundary

Green Shale is at the documented concept, architecture and validation-planning stage.

External research informs the proposed testing routes. It does not establish Green Shale-specific product performance.

The portfolio does not claim completed industrial qualification, certified products, confirmed offtake, realised returns or verified carbon-credit eligibility.
